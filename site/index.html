Meet Jay

Jayla Stanford, Jay to her regulars, started selling her desserts around Houston while studying at Prairie View A&M. In 2025 she put them on wheels: a pink truck that pulls up Friday to Sunday with cheesecake by the cup, the slice and the cone.
